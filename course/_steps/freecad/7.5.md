FreeCAD 1.1 has no clash detection tool; a short console check with FreeCAD's solid intersection finds the clashes, and Remove Component cuts the openings, which FreeCAD exports as IfcOpeningElement.

1. Open `tb01.FCStd` (sections 1 and 2 are what matters here).
2. 3D/BIM > Generic 3D Tools > External Reference, **File** `datasets/mep_model.ifc`. Rename it `MEP model` and keep it outside TB-01 Training Building.
3. View > Panels > Python Console. Run:
   ```python
   doc = FreeCAD.ActiveDocument
   ref = doc.getObjectsByLabel("MEP model")[0]
   els = [o for o in doc.Objects if getattr(o, "IfcType", "") in ("Beam", "Column", "Wall", "Slab")]
   for i, duct in enumerate(ref.Shape.Solids):
       for e in els:
           v = duct.common(e.Shape).Volume
           if v > 1:
               print("duct", i, "clashes with", e.Label, round(v / 1e6, 1), "litres")
   ```
   It lists five clashes: duct 0 (D-1, x 2.85 to 3.15 m, z +3.100 to +3.250) with B-1/AB, B-2/AB, B-3/AB, and duct 1 (D-2, y 10.35 to 10.65 m, z +2.600 to +2.750) with W-Core-W and W-Core-E.
4. Opening for B-1/AB: 3D/BIM > Generic 3D Tools > Box, then in the Data tab **Length** `360 mm`, **Width** `400 mm`, **Height** `210 mm`, **Placement > Position** 2820, -200, 3070. That is the duct plus 30 mm all round (25 mm clearance and a 5 mm margin), through the full beam width.
5. Select the box, then Ctrl+click B-1/AB, Modify > Remove Component.
6. Same for B-2/AB (Position 2820, 6800, 3070) and B-3/AB (Position 2820, 13800, 3070).
7. Openings in the walls for D-2: boxes **Length** `350 mm`, **Width** `360 mm`, **Height** `210 mm` at 7450, 10320, 2570 (W-Core-W) and 10200, 10320, 2570 (W-Core-E); Remove Component on each wall.
8. Run the script of step 3 again: it prints nothing. Each beam keeps more than 95 % of its volume.
9. Select TB-01 Training Building, File > Export…, `structure.ifc`. Do not move the ducts and do not export the MEP model.
