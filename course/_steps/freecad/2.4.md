FreeCAD places a beam on the line between the two points you give, with its section centred on that line. For a 350 mm downstand under the soffit (+3.000 to +3.350) the line is at mid-depth, z = 3175 mm.

1. Open `tb01.FCStd`. Right-click **Level 1** and tick **Active** (new beams go into Level 1).
2. 3D/BIM > Beam. In the task panel set **Width** `300 mm` and **Height** `350 mm`. Tick **Global** so the points you type are model coordinates, and tick **Continue**.
3. Beam B-1/AB on grid 1 between the faces of C-A1 and C-B1: type X `200`, Y `0`, Z `3175`, Enter; then X `5800`, Y `0`, Z `3175`, Enter.
4. Draw the other beams along X the same way: from x+200 to the next grid x-200, at y = 0, 7000 and 14000 (9 beams in total).
5. Beams along Y: from y+200 to the next grid y-200, at x = 0, 6000, 12000, 18000, for example B-A/12 from 0, 200, 3175 to 0, 6800, 3175 (8 beams).
6. Press Esc. Rename the beams `B-1/AB`, `B-1/BC`, `B-1/CD`, `B-2/AB` ... `B-D/23`: grid line, then the two grids of the bay.
7. Select one beam and check the Data tab: **Ifc Type** `Beam`, **Length** `5600 mm` (along X) or `6600 mm` (along Y), **Width** `300 mm`, **Height** `350 mm`.
8. Check that the 17 beams are inside Level 1. Clear **Active**, save and export `tb01.ifc`.

If this does not work: if a beam stands on end, you clicked instead of typing the second point at the same Z; delete it and type both points.
