1. Open `tb01.FCStd`. Right-click **Level 0** and tick **Active**. Click an empty part of the 3D view so nothing is selected.
2. 3D/BIM > Stairs. A straight flight appears at the origin.
3. In the Data tab set **Length** `5320 mm` (19 treads of 280), **Width** `1200 mm`, **Height** `3600 mm` and **Number Of Steps** `20`. Check that **Riser Height** shows `180 mm` and **Tread Depth** `280 mm`.
4. Leave **Align** at `Left` and **Structure** at `Massive`. Set **Placement**: **Angle** `90 °`, **Axis** 0, 0, 1, **Position** 13000, 1000, 0. The flight now runs in +Y from y 1.0 m, between x 13.0 and 14.2 m.
5. Rename it `ST-1` and check that it is inside Level 0.
6. Look at the top step: FreeCAD models 19 treads, up to +3.420. The 20th riser is the edge of the slab opening at Level 1, and the flight ends at y 6.32, inside the opening.
7. Store the riser count: select ST-1, Manage > Manage IFC Properties. In **Add property set** pick **Stair Common**. Set **NumberOfRiser** `20` and **NumberOfTreads** `19` and leave the other values empty. Press OK.
8. Do not fill **RiserHeight** there: FreeCAD 1.1.3 writes length properties in millimetres into a file whose unit is the metre, so 180 would be read as 180 m. The checker takes the riser height from the geometry.
9. Clear **Active** on Level 0. Save and export `tb01.ifc`.
