Bonsai's parametric stair counts treads, not risers: 19 treads give 20 risers of 3.6 / 20 = 0.180 m. It also draws a full tread at the top, so you shorten the last one to land at y 6.4.

1. Make **Level 0** the default container.
2. From the **Add** menu in the 3D viewport header, choose **IFC Element**. In the dialog set **Name** `ST-1`, **Definition** IfcElement, **Class** IfcStairFlight, **Predefined Type** STRAIGHT, **Representation** Stair, and click OK.
3. Go to **Geometry and Materials > Parametric Geometry > Stair** and click the pencil (**Enable Editing Stair**).
4. Set **Stair Type** Concrete, **Width** `1.2`, **Height** `3.6`, **Number of Treads** `19`, **Tread Run** `0.28`, **Tread Depth** `0.25`, **Base Slab Depth** `0.25`, **Top Slab Depth** `0.25`.
5. Untick **Has Top Nib**. With it on, the waist runs on under the landing to y 6.67, past the slab edge.
6. Click **Unlock First/Last Treads** and set **Custom First / Last Treads Widths** to `0.28` and `0.08`. The last riser is then at y 6.32 and the top tread ends at y 6.40.
7. Click **Finish Editing**. The panel shows **Number of Risers** 20, **Tread Rise** 0.18 and **Length** 5.40.
8. Bonsai builds the flight along its local X, with the width along local Y. In the sidebar (N) > **Item** set **Location** `14.2`, `1.0`, `0` and **Rotation Z** `90`. The flight now climbs in +Y between x 13.0 and 14.2.
9. Look at the stair in **Object Information > Object > Property Sets**: Bonsai filled **Pset_StairFlightCommon** with NumberOfRiser 20 and RiserHeight 0.18.
10. Check from the side that the top tread meets the slab edge at +3.600. Save with **File > Save IFC Project**.
