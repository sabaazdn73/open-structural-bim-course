A Bonsai project library is any IFC file you append types from. Your template starts empty and takes its types from `tb01.ifc`, with their profiles and materials.

1. Choose **File > New IFC Project > New Metric (m) Project**. Rename the project `TB-01 Structural Template` in **Object Attributes**.
2. In **Spatial Decomposition**, either delete **My Storey** (click it and **X**, **Delete Container**) or rename it and add the five levels as in 1.2. Any level you keep must have a name from the brief.
3. Go to **Project Overview > Project Setup > Project Library**. Click the folder icon (**Select Library File**) and pick your `tb01.ifc`.
4. Open the **IfcColumnType** category in the list and click the append icon (**Append Library Element**) next to **RC Column 400x400**.
5. Append **RC Beam 300x350 downstand** from IfcBeamType and **Pad Footing 1800x1800x600** from IfcFootingType. Their profiles and materials (Concrete C30/37, Concrete C25/30) come with them.
6. In **Geometry and Materials > Materials**, check the list and add `A500` (Category `steel`) with **+** (**Add Material**). Add any concrete that is missing.
7. Do not place any element. The template holds only definitions.
8. Save with **File > Save IFC Project** as `template.ifc`.
9. Next time, start a project with **File > Open IFC Project** on a copy of `template.ifc`, or keep your project and append from the template in **Project Library**.
