Bonsai has no separate callout object. A detail is a drawing with a small frame and a large scale; Bonsai marks section drawings on the other drawings by itself.

1. Save the model. Set the **3D Cursor Location** to X `0`, Y `0`, Z `-0.4` (the centre line of C-A1 and F-A1).
2. In **Drawings and Documents > Drawings**, add a **Section** looking **North** (**+**, **Add Drawing**). Rename it `TYPICAL COLUMN FOOTING DETAIL` and activate it.
3. In **Active Drawing**, set **Width** `3`, **Height** `2.4`, **Depth** `1` and **Scale** 1:20. The frame shows F-A1 and the foot of C-A1 with their bars.
4. Pick the **Annotation Tool**, choose **Text** and click **Add** (Shift+A). A text appears near the cursor.
5. Press Shift+E (**Edit Text**). Replace the literal "TEXT" with `TYPICAL COLUMN FOOTING DETAIL` and click OK. Move the text above the footing with G.
6. Add four more texts the same way:
   - `CONCRETE C25/30`
   - `STEEL A500`
   - `BOTTOM MESH Ø16 @ 150 EACH WAY` (copy the Ø from this page if your keyboard has none)
   - `50 mm COVER`
7. If you want leaders, choose **Leader** in the Annotation Tool and add one per note; its text works the same way.
8. Click **Create Drawing**. Bonsai writes `drawings/TYPICAL COLUMN FOOTING DETAIL.svg`. Copy it as `detail.svg`.
