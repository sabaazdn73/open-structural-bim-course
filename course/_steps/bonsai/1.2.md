1. Open `tb01.ifc` with **File > Open IFC Project** if it is not open.
2. Go to **Project Overview > Spatial > Spatial Decomposition**. The list shows the project, site, building and My Storey. The right column of each storey is its elevation.
3. Double-click **My Storey** in the list and rename it `Level 0`. Leave its elevation at 0.
4. Click the row **TB-01** (the building). In the dropdown above the list choose **IfcBuildingStorey** and click the **+** button (**Add Part to Object**). A new storey appears under the building.
5. Double-click the new storey's name and type `Foundation`. Double-click its elevation and type `-1.2`. Bonsai moves the storey to that height.
6. Repeat steps 4 and 5 for `Level 1` at `3.6`, `Level 2` at `7.2` and `Roof` at `10.8`.
7. Check that the five storeys are listed under TB-01 and nothing else is.
8. Click the **Level 0** row and click **Set Default**. New elements go into the default storey, and the panel header shows "Default: Level 0".
9. Save with **File > Save IFC Project**.

If this does not work: if a storey ends up under the site or the project, delete it with the **X** button (**Delete Container**), click the building row again and add it once more.
