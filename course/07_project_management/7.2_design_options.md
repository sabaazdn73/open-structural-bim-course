# Exercise 7.2: Design options: compare two versions

**Goal.** Save two design options and compare them.

## What you build

- Option A: your TB-01 model as it is (sections 1 and 2).
- Option B: the same model with beam **B-2/BC** changed to 400 wide x 500 deep, still hung from the slab soffit (+2.850 to +3.350).
- Save them as option_A.ifc and option_B.ifc, then compare them with your program's IFC diff tool.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `option_A.ifc, option_B.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 7.2 option_A.ifc option_B.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

Revit keeps options inside one file; in open BIM you keep each option as its own file (or Git branch) and compare them. The comparison is the deliverable: exactly what changed, and nothing else.
