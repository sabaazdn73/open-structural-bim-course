# Exercise 7.5: Clash detection with the MEP model

**Goal.** Find the clashes between your structure and the MEP model, and resolve them with openings.

## What you build

- Run a clash check between your TB-01 model (sections 1 and 2) and `datasets/mep_model.ifc`.
- Duct D-1 (300 x 150) crosses three beams on grid A-B; duct D-2 (300 x 150) crosses two core walls.
- Resolve each clash with an opening in the beam or wall, at least 25 mm clear of the duct on every side. Do not move the ducts: the MEP model is not yours.
- Keep openings local: each beam keeps at least 90% of its volume.
- Save as structure.ifc.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `structure.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 7.5 structure.ifc datasets/mep_model.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

Clash detection is what Navisworks is used for, and it is the daily work of BIM coordinators (and, for example, of the BIM teams that model supports and anchors for MEP). Any opening in a beam must be agreed with the structural engineer; this exercise sizes them, a real project also checks them.
