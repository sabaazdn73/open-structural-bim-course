# Exercise 7.8: Export for issue: IFC4, levels, base quantities

**Goal.** Export the finished TB-01 model for issue, with base quantities.

## What you build

- Schema IFC4, SI units.
- Every element in a level.
- Base quantities (Qto_...BaseQuantities with the volume) on every column, beam, slab, wall and footing, matching the geometry.
- Save as tb01_issue.ifc.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `tb01_issue.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 7.8 tb01_issue.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

The issued IFC is what the client, the contractor and the checker receive. Export settings decide whether quantities, properties and the spatial structure arrive intact.
