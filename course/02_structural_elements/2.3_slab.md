# Exercise 2.3: Level 1 slab with openings

**Goal.** Model the 250 mm Level 1 slab with the core and stair openings.

## What you build

- Top of slab +3.600, soffit +3.350.
- Edges at the outer column faces: x -0.2 to 18.2, y -0.2 to 14.2.
- Core opening x 7.75 to 10.25, y 9.25 to 11.75; stair opening x 13.0 to 14.2, y 1.0 to 6.4.
- Place the slab in Level 1. Expected volume: 63.06 m3.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `tb01.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 2.3 tb01.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

Openings must be real openings (voids) in the slab, not holes drawn on top of it, or the quantities and every downstream check will be wrong. The checker compares the slab's volume with the brief.
