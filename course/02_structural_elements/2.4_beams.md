# Exercise 2.4: Level 1 beams

**Goal.** Model the 17 downstand beams of Level 1.

## What you build

- One beam on every grid line between two columns: 9 along X (grids 1, 2, 3) and 8 along Y (grids A to D).
- 300 mm wide, 350 mm deep below the slab soffit (+3.000 to +3.350).
- Each beam stops at the column faces (clear spans: 5.6 m along X, 6.6 m along Y).
- Name them by grid line and bay: B-1/AB is the beam on grid 1 between A and B. Place them in Level 1.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `tb01.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 2.4 tb01.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

The full beam is 600 mm deep (350 downstand + 250 slab). Modelling only the downstand is a common convention that keeps the concrete volumes honest. Stopping beams at the column faces does the same at the joints.
