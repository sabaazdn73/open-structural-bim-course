# Exercise 2.2: Core walls traced from the architect's model

**Goal.** Link the architect's model and model the structural core walls exactly where the architect placed their load-bearing walls.

## What you build

- Open or link `datasets/architect_model.ifc` as a reference. It holds four load-bearing core walls (A-Core-1 to 4) and two partitions that are not yours to model.
- Model four 250 mm walls on the architect's core walls: outside faces x 7.5 to 10.5, y 9.0 to 12.0.
- From +0.000 to the slab soffit +3.350, in Level 0.
- Mark them load-bearing (`Pset_WallCommon.LoadBearing = True`).

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `tb01.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 2.2 tb01.ifc datasets/architect_model.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

On a real project you never type the architect's coordinates in again: you link their model and trace (or copy and monitor) the elements that are structural. Linking also shows you at once when the architect moves a wall. Partitions stay in the architect's model.
