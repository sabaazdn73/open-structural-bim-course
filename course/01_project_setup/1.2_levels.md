# Exercise 1.2: Levels (building storeys)

**Goal.** Create the five levels of TB-01 at their structural elevations.

## What you build

- Foundation **-1.200**, Level 0 **+0.000**, Level 1 **+3.600**, Level 2 **+7.200**, Roof **+10.800**.
- Use exactly these names; they are how the checker (and your colleagues) find the levels.
- All levels belong to the building TB-01.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `tb01.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 1.2 tb01.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

In IFC a level is an *IfcBuildingStorey*: every element is placed in one, and schedules, drawings and quantities are organised by it. Structural levels are usually at the top of the structural slab, which is a few centimetres below the architect's finished floor level; agree which one you model before you start.
