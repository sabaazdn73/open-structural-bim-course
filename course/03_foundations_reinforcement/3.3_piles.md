# Exercise 3.3: Pile cap and piles under the core

**Goal.** Support the core on a pile cap and four piles.

## What you build

- Pile cap PC-1: 4.0 x 4.0 x 1.0 m centred at x 9.0, y 10.5, top -0.600; footing type PILE_CAP.
- Four piles (IfcPile), 600 mm diameter, 12 m long, at 1.2 m each way from the core centre, from the underside of the cap (-1.600) down to -13.600.
- Everything in the Foundation level.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `tb01.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 3.3 tb01.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

Piles and caps show how IFC separates element classes (IfcPile, IfcFooting) and predefined types (PILE_CAP, PAD_FOOTING). Getting the class right is what lets a contractor's software pick up the piles for the piling subcontract.
