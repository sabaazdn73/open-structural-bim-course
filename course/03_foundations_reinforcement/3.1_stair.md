# Exercise 3.1: Stair from Level 0 to Level 1

**Goal.** Model a straight concrete stair through the stair opening.

## What you build

- Width 1.2 m between x 13.0 and 14.2; first riser at y 1.0; climbs in +Y.
- 20 risers of 180 mm (total rise 3.600), treads of 280 mm, arriving at Level 1 inside the opening (y up to 6.4).
- Place it in Level 0. If your program can store the number of risers and the riser height, do it.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `tb01.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 3.1 tb01.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

Stairs are where architecture and structure meet: the structural engineer owns the flight and its supports, the architect owns the going and the headroom. A stair modelled with the right riser count is also checked against building rules (riser height, going) by code-checking tools.
