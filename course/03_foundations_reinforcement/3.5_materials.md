# Exercise 3.5: Materials and concrete data

**Goal.** Give every element its material and its concrete data.

## What you build

- Columns, beams, slab and core walls: material **Concrete C30/37**.
- Pad footings, the pile cap and the piles: **Concrete C25/30**.
- On every concrete element, `Pset_ConcreteElementGeneral`: StrengthClass (C30/37 or C25/30) and ExposureClass (XC1 above ground, XC2 for footings, cap and piles).

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `tb01.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 3.5 tb01.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

The material drives the quantities by concrete class and the colours in views; the property set is what a reviewer or an automatic check reads (see the model-checking notes in this repository). A model without this data looks finished but cannot be checked.
