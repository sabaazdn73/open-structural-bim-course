# Exercise 3.4: Reinforcement: column C-A1 and footing F-A1

**Goal.** Reinforce one column and one footing with real bars.

## What you build

- Steel A500.
- Column C-A1: 8 vertical Ø16 bars (corners and mid-sides) over the column height; Ø8 links at 150 mm from 50 mm above Level 0; 30 mm nominal cover to the links (so the vertical bars are 38 mm from the faces).
- Footing F-A1: bottom mesh Ø16 at 150 mm both ways (12 bars each way), 50 mm cover.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `tb01.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 3.4 tb01.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

Reinforcement is where BIM pays off for a concrete engineer: bar schedules, bending shapes and clash checks between bars and inserts come straight from the model. It is also the part of the model that the site checks most carefully before a pour.
