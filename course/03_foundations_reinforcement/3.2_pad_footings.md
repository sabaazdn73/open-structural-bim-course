# Exercise 3.2: Pad footings

**Goal.** Place a pad footing under every column.

## What you build

- 1.8 x 1.8 x 0.6 m, centred under each column, top at -0.600, bottom at -1.200.
- Footing type PAD_FOOTING, named F-A1 ... F-D3, in the Foundation level.
- The short column stubs between the footings and Level 0 are not part of this course.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `tb01.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 3.2 tb01.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

Foundations are their own discipline in the model: their level, their concrete (C25/30 here) and their quantities are reported separately.
