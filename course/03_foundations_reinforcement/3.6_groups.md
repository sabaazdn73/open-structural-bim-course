## Check your work

Save or export your work as `tb01.ifc`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 3.6 tb01.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

Groups (IfcGroup) are saved selections that travel with the model: another engineer can open the IFC and select the same set. They are the open equivalent of Revit's selection sets.
