# Exercise 5.2: Section through grid 2

**Goal.** Cut a section through grid 2 and annotate the levels.

## What you build

- Section along grid 2 looking north, showing columns, beams, the slab and the footings.
- Level lines with names and elevations: Foundation -1.200, Level 0 +0.000, Level 1 +3.600.
- Grid bubbles A to D. Export as SVG or PDF.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `section.svg`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 5.2 section.svg
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

Sections show what plans hide: levels, depths and how elements stack. Level tags read their values from the model, so they update when a level moves.
