# Exercise 5.4: Concrete quantity schedule (CSV)

**Goal.** Schedule the concrete volume of every element and export it to CSV.

## What you build

- One row per element (or per class) with the element class and its volume in m3.
- Columns, beams, slab, walls, footings (pad and cap), piles.
- Export as CSV. The checker compares your totals with the volumes in your model (2% tolerance).

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `schedule.csv`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 5.4 schedule.csv tb01.ifc
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

This is the quantity takeoff an estimator does by hand from drawings, done in minutes from the model. It only works if the model is clean (exercise 2.5) and every element has its class right.
