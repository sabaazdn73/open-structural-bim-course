# Exercise 5.1: Level 1 framing plan with grids and dimensions

**Goal.** Produce the Level 1 framing plan from the model and export it as SVG (or PDF).

## What you build

- A plan view of Level 1 showing the grid with its bubbles (A to D, 1 to 3), columns, beams and the slab openings.
- Dimension the bays (6000 x 3 along X, 7000 x 2 along Y) and the overall sizes (18000 and 14000).
- Export as SVG (best) or PDF with text kept as text.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `plan.svg`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 5.1 plan.svg
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

The drawing is still the legal document on most projects. A plan cut from the model stays right when the model changes; a plan drawn by hand does not.
