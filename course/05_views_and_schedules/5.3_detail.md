# Exercise 5.3: Detail callout with annotation

**Goal.** Make a detail of footing F-A1 with notes.

## What you build

- A callout (enlarged view) of footing F-A1 with the column above.
- Title **TYPICAL COLUMN FOOTING DETAIL**.
- Notes: concrete **C25/30**, steel **A500**, bottom mesh **Ø16 @ 150** each way, **50 mm cover**.
- Export as SVG or PDF.

All numbers come from the [project brief](../../brief/README.md) and its [drawings](../../brief/drawings/).

## Check your work

Save or export your work as `detail.svg`, then run:

```
python -m bimcheck 5.3 detail.svg
```

Every line should say PASS. A FAIL line tells you what was expected and what was found.

## Why this matters

Details are where 2D annotation adds what the model does not show well. Keep notes short and tied to the model's data so they do not contradict it.
